## Introduction

**1013D** is a high-performance online 3D viewer designed for 3D printing enthusiasts and engineers. It solves the common problem of "scale anxiety" by allowing users to calibrate their screens to exact physical dimensions, enabling true **1:1 scale visualization** of STL, OBJ, and 3MF files before printing.

Beyond standard viewing, 1013D features a built-in **Augmented Reality (AR)** engine, allowing you to instantly project your models into the real world using iOS AR Quick Look or Android Scene Viewer.

## Key Features

- **True 1:1 Physical Scale**
  Calibrate your monitor's PPI (Pixels Per Inch) by entering your screen size or alignment with a standard ID card (ISO 7810). Once calibrated, the 3D model on your screen matches its exact real-world dimensions.

- **Instant AR Preview**
  Generate AR-compatible files instantly. View your models in your physical room using a mobile device or AR headset.

- **Immersive Dark UI**
  A clean, modern interface designed with a true black background and vibrant accents, making it easy to view fine structural details on your assets.

- **Broad Format Support**
  Native, client-side processing for **STL**, **OBJ**, and **3MF** files.
