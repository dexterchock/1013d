How to view 3D models in 1:1 scale

  1. Upload your .STL, .OBJ, or .3MF file.
  2. Select "Calibrate" from the menu.
  3. Type in your screen resolution and size for the PPI Calibration for precision, or use manual calibration by placing your Credit Card to the screen for a 1:1 match.
  4. Click the 1:1 button at the bottom right. Your display now shows the model at real-world dimensions.
